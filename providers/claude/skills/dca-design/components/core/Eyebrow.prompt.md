Eyebrow — small uppercase overline that signals the section or operational domain (Logistics, Operations, Fundraising) above a heading.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Logistics</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow color="orange">Urgent appeal</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow color="muted" tick={false}>Operational report</Eyebrow>
```

Props: `color` (blue | orange | yellow | muted), `tick` (leading colour bar, default true).
