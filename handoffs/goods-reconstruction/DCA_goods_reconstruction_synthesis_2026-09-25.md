Two bounded findings follow. First, box 24 is blank on all thirteen sender copies: the *processed set* holds no consignee receipt — which is expected for sender copies and says nothing about whether signed copies exist elsewhere in DCA's files (Gmail attachments, the stamped August set, returned scans, physical archives) or with the consignees and carriers, none of which were searched. Second, pallet numbers are per-document positions: identical-composition entries carry different numbers across the May working file's own lists and across months, so a number alone cannot carry identity; weight and composition give candidate matches in these sources, and other evidence (labels, photographs, a persistent logistics-unit identifier) could establish physical identity where these sources cannot. Two further points are kept deliberately open: CMR places of delivery are declared destinations, not proof of unloading; and the May NR note's declaration of hygiene within its 1,510 kg is consistent with the proposed reading of James's May tab but does not show how he counted the 572 kg.
